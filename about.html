About JustSilver

SILVER WITH A SECOND LIFE.

JustSilver is an independent shop for vintage sterling silver jewelry. We select pieces for their design and craftsmanship, with an emphasis on fair pricing.

We want to make well-made vintage jewelry easier to find and give each piece a chance to be worn again.

Fair. Just. Silver.
